## How to read the columns

- **Max curvature**: sharpest bend on the path. Above the car's max curvature (0.195 1/m) the car cannot follow the path.
- **Over-limit length**: metres of path that bend more sharply than the car can steer.
- **Footprint hits**: path poses where the full car rectangle would touch an obstacle (A* only checks a disc around the rear axle).
- **Goal heading error**: difference between the car's final heading and the heading the parking manoeuvre needs. Grid A* has no notion of heading.
